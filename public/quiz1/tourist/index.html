Iconic Heritage and Destinations in Surabaya
Maritime Heritage
Monumen Kapal Selam (Monkasel)

A genuine Soviet-built Whiskey-class submarine (KRI Pasopati 410) that participated in the 1962 Trikora operation. Displayed prominently along the Kalimas River, it is the largest submarine monument in Asia where visitors can tour the torpedo bays and interior command center.

Cultural Promenade
Jalan Tunjungan

Surabaya's historic urban corridor immortalized in the classic song "Mlaku-Mlaku Nang Tunjungan". Features preserved Dutch Art Deco architecture, illuminated heritage facades, evening street musicians, pedestrian walkways, and lively coffee spots.

Colonial Landmark
House of Sampoerna

A grand 1862 Dutch colonial compound with stately columns, once an orphanage and later an industrial base. It now houses a preservation museum displaying historical artifacts, a classic art gallery, and cultural exhibits celebrating Surabaya's commercial history.
